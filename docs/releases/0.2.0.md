# leenef 0.2.0 release notes

## Highlights

- Expanded the activation catalog for analytic NEF layers with small
  nonlinearities and spectrum probes including `bent_line`, `double_fold`,
  `sqrt_abs`, `cos`, `gauss`, `cos_sin`, `square_wave`, `floor_wave`, `box`,
  `gelu`, `silu`, `softplus`, `linear`, `step`, and `square`.
- Added activation-spectrum benchmark and analysis tooling: Hermite-mode
  recovery, kink-angle sweeps, full-spectrum/digital activation panels, and
  throttled runners suitable for long CPU jobs.
- Added abs-preimage geometry helpers in `leenef.preimages` for exposing the
  affine encoder, solving fixed-sign activity preimages, checking class-target
  preimages, and computing null-space bases.
- Published two standalone adjunct papers:
  - `docs/activation_spectra.md` / `.pdf`, on the kernel and Hermite-spectrum
    role of pointwise nonlinearities in analytic NEF layers.
  - `docs/abs_preimage_geometry.md` / `.pdf`, on the local sign-cell geometry
    induced by the default `abs` activation.
- Cross-referenced the SSUP paper series and adjunct papers, refreshed PDFs,
  and moved Literature before appendices across the paper set.

## Notes for users

- The default activation remains `abs`; the new activation set is primarily
  for controlled comparisons and research. Smooth full-spectrum activations
  such as `gelu`/`silu` and digital/RFF-like activations are now available via
  the existing `activation=` argument.
- `floor_wave` is available as an integer-aligned digital diagnostic
  activation.
- The new preimage helpers are diagnostic utilities for `abs`-activation
  layers, not a replacement for the standard `NEFLayer.fit` workflow.

## Notes for readers

- SSUP-I remains the supervised-learning anchor, but its activation discussion
  now points to the activation-spectrum adjunct for the general Hermite/kernel
  treatment in `docs/activation_spectra.pdf`.
- The abs-preimage adjunct complements the spectrum paper by explaining the
  geometric consequences of the default sign-folding activation in
  `docs/abs_preimage_geometry.pdf`.
- The release keeps repository claims tied to rebuilt PDFs and
  code-identity-tagged experiment artifacts.
